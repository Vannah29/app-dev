
# My Favorite Movie: Inside Out 2

## Introduction
My favorite movie is **Inside Out 2**. It is an animated movie that shows different emotions and experiences while growing up.

## About the Movie
*Inside Out 2* is a movie about Riley and her emotions. It introduces new emotions that affect her life as she becomes a teenager.

## My Favorite Characters
Here are my favorite characters:

- Joy
- Sadness
- Anxiety
- Anger
- Disgust

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it teaches us that **all emotions are important**. It also reminds me that it is okay to feel sad, nervous, and happy.

## My Favorite Lesson
> Every emotion has a purpose, and we should learn to accept ourselves.

## My Favorite Scene
My favorite scene is when Riley learns to accept her emotions and understand herself better.
